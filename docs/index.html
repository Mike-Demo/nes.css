#Foundations

Color roles

  • Primary drives action and focus.
  • Success communicates safe automation.
  • Warning slows users before risky steps.
  • Error reserves attention for broken trust.

Interaction model

  • Expose model, mode, and confidence near every action.
  • Favor visible system status over hidden background work.
  • Make approval, retry, and rollback obvious.

Documentation voice

  • Short, instructive labels
  • Operational language over marketing-only copy
  • Explain what the AI did and why it is safe
